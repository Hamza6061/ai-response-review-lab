Scoring rubric — read before reviewing

Accuracy: correct facts and reasoning, grounded in the supplied evidence.
Relevance: directly addresses the question.
Clarity: easy to understand and logically expressed.
Instruction following: meets explicit format and scope requirements.

1 = major failure; 2 = substantial problems; 3 = mixed, needs revision; 4 = minor issues; 5 = fully meets this criterion. Score dimensions independently. A fluent incorrect answer can score well on clarity.

Prefer the answer that best satisfies the task overall, prioritising correctness and explicit instructions. Choose Tie for comparable quality, or Both poor when neither answer is acceptable. Cite a concrete difference in your rationale.
